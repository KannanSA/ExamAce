# ExamAce Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

ExamAce does not collect, sell, or use personal data for tracking or advertising.

Study sessions, timer progress, streaks, points, and planner preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to a developer-controlled server.

The current release does not create an online account and does not sync tutor, HealthKit, calendar, or study data to a developer-controlled service. If a future release enables optional integrations, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that release.

You can remove locally stored ExamAce data by deleting the app. For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/KannanSA/ExamAce/issues.
